# 📊 Employee Attrition Analysis using Python, MySQL & Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

Employee attrition is one of the biggest challenges faced by organizations, as high employee turnover can increase recruitment costs, reduce productivity, and affect overall business performance. This project aims to analyze employee attrition data to identify the key factors influencing employee turnover and provide actionable business insights.

The project follows a complete data analytics workflow, starting from data cleaning and preprocessing in Python, storing the cleaned dataset in a MySQL database, and finally creating interactive dashboards in Power BI for visualization and decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze employee attrition patterns.
- Clean and preprocess HR data using Python.
- Store the cleaned dataset in MySQL.
- Connect MySQL with Power BI.
- Develop interactive HR dashboards.
- Identify the major drivers of employee attrition.
- Generate business insights and recommendations for improving employee retention.

## 💡 Business Insights

The analysis revealed several important findings:

- Overtime is strongly associated with higher employee attrition.
- Employees in lower job levels tend to leave more frequently.
- Early-career employees show higher turnover.
- Certain departments and job roles experience significantly higher attrition.
- Promotion delays and lower salaries contribute to employee turnover.

---

## ✅ Business Recommendations

- Improve work-life balance by reducing excessive overtime.
- Provide structured career growth opportunities.
- Implement competitive salary and reward programs.
- Increase employee engagement initiatives.
- Conduct regular employee satisfaction surveys.
- Strengthen retention strategies for high-risk employee groups.
